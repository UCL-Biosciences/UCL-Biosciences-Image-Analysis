Brief overview of data used to perform segmentation:

1. BBBC035 dataset: Simulated HL60 cells (from the Cell Tracking Challenge) - https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/BBBC035
  Data has ground truth annotation as foreground

2. S-BIAD1272, 30min_stimulation, 240109_240110_S1_30min_pMAPK_EGF - https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/bioimages/studies/S-BIAD1272?query=3D%2C%20confocal
  Dataset is publicly available as LIF. We converted the files to TIF with a custom fiji macro. Five of these .tif files are included in the repo to allow users to test the 3D notebook.
  Reproduced from Claudia Catapano, Marina S. Dietz & Mike Heilemann (2024). Confocal microscopy data of phospho-p44/42 MAPK (Erk1/2) (Thr202/Tyr204) in HeLa cells stimulated with EGF or HGF. BioStudies, S-BIAD1272. Retrieved from https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/bioimages/studies/S-BIAD1272
